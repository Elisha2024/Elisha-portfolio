# Personal identity explorations

Three original SVG constructions were compared at 16px, 32px, and navigation size, in monochrome and on light/dark surfaces. These are visual design judgements, not measured audience research or trademark clearance.

| Concept | Originality | Readability | Scalability | Visual balance | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared stem | Custom lowercase em ligature | Recognizable initials at large size | Junctions crowd at favicon size | Friendly but right-heavy | Retain as typographic alternative |
| Confluence | Three paths converge through shared curvature | Abstract, deliberate silhouette | Simple strokes survive at 16px | Consistent weight and generous negative space | Selected |
| Fold | E-shaped field and intersecting ribbon | Strong E, less explicit M | Dense central overlap | Strong but visually rigid | Retain as geometric alternative |

Confluence refers to backend systems, data, and intelligence meeting in one direction. It has no generic code brackets, container, hexagon, or circuit-board nodes. The primary mark uses currentColor and works in one color. It does not require animation. A short hover rotation adds feedback only.

`concepts.svg` is the review board. The original three standalone SVG files are preserved here. `src/components/brand.tsx` and `public/icon.svg` implement the chosen geometry. Carswafi's live CSS at https://carswafi.com/_next/static/chunks/3ir0u_6kwzdnq.css was inspected on 2026-10-23: its --color-secondary is #0078ff; its --color-primary is mint #78fcc5. The portfolio deliberately uses the blue as its own primary inspiration. No Carswafi logo or mint identity is copied.

## Current symbol-only refinement

The user subsequently requested a logo instead of the visible name. `04-em.svg` is now implemented: an original EM ligature built from two balanced open paths, drawn as vectors rather than a font inside a container. It appears alone in the navbar and footer. The links have accessible home labels. The favicon and social asset use the same geometry. The earlier three-concept board and Confluence component are retained as historical alternatives.

## Woven em — current direction

The latest request asked for more character than the uppercase EM. 05-woven-em.svg is now implemented: a custom open-loop e shares its right-hand junction with a pair of m arches. White and blue distinguish the two strokes on dark surfaces. The two-tone treatment follows the reference's restraint; the geometry and initials are original. The earlier 04 mark remains an alternative.
