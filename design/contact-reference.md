# Contact page reference alignment

Reference: https://conrad-otieno-product-designer.netlify.app/ — open its Contact view using the navigation button. Inspected in Chromium at desktop and 390px widths on 23 October 2026.

The supplied reference uses a centred introduction above two equal-width, equal-height light cards. Contact rows use circular line icons and have no separators between the first three entries; response time has a separate top rule. The form uses vertically stacked full-width fields and a full-width rounded submit button. The contact view ends in a compact dark footer rather than another contact CTA.

Recreated that structure with Elisha's verified email and location, the user-provided availability/response copy, original logo, and blue accents. Original contact success feedback and mail forwarding behavior are preserved. Copy email and LinkedIn remain compact utilities rather than large additional CTAs. No reference logo, portrait, personal address, or source assets were copied.

Desktop treatment: 1080px content width, 64px card gap, 48px card padding, 16px corners, Montserrat 72px/75.6px hero at weight 600, 36px/54px card headings at 600, 14px/20px labels at 500, 16px/24px inputs at 400, 50px inputs, 170px textarea, and a 52px submit button. Phones use centred 40px/42px headings, 28px/42px card headings, 32px card padding, and a single column with 48px between cards. Small-phone padding adapts to 24px.
